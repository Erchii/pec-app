About PEC-ML

Diploma thesis — Photoelectrochemical water splitting prediction using machine learning.

Prediction Targets

  • Photocurrent Density — mA/cm² — 410 training samples
  • STH Efficiency — % — 48 training samples
  • H₂ Evolution Rate — µmol/h/cm² — 57 training samples

Features Used

  • Numeric: bandgap, pH, temperature, bias, light intensity, thickness + 8 engineered
  • Categorical: material, electrolyte, synthesis method, photoelectrode type, nanostructure, morphology

Anti-Leakage

  • Photocurrent is excluded from STH model features (STH ≈ J·1.23/P — physics leakage)
  • log1p transform applied to H₂ (right-skewed distribution)
